CORPORATE PROFILE

A Legacy of Power & Precision in High-Pressure Engineering

Krishna Enterprises is a trusted service provider specializing in the Operation & Maintenance (O&M) of power plants. With extensive expertise in the power generation sector, we deliver reliable, efficient, and safety-focused solutions for Gas Turbines, Steam Turbines, and Combined Cycle units.

Headquartered in Muzaffarnagar (Uttar Pradesh), our technical corps combines over 18 years of field overhauls, predictive vibration diagnostics, heat-rate optimization, and environmental FGD scrubbing to ensure your generation assets achieve maximum availability and compliance with statutory Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR 1950).
