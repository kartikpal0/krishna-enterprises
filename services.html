Our 9 Core Industrial O&M Services

Full-spectrum operational expertise spanning captive co-gen plants, utility IPPs, steam & gas turbine generator overhauls, and FGD emission systems.

01 TURNKEY OPERATIONS

O&M of Captive & Independent Power Plants

Comprehensive operation and maintenance services for captive co-generation and independent power plants across India. Our certified teams handle 24/7 DCS control rooms, preventive maintenance, and heat-rate optimization to maintain availability SLAs above 99.2%.

Key Deliverables: Continuous shift monitoring, thermodynamic combustion tuning, fuel management, water chemistry control.
Predictive Maintenance: FFT vibration spectrum analysis, lube oil centrifugation, thermal imaging of motor terminals.

02 TURBINE OVERHAUL

AMC & Overhauling of Steam and Gas Turbine Gen Sets

Specialized maintenance and overhaul services for steam and gas turbine generator sets. We conduct precision rotor uncoupling, blade NDT inspections, dynamic balancing, and emergency trip governor tests.

Turbine Makes Handled: Triveni Turbines, BHEL, Siemens, extraction/condensing sets up to 200 MW.
Clearance Tolerances: Journal & thrust bearing oil clearance checks, labyrinth seal renewals, diaphragm blast cleaning.
Request Proposal for This Service
03 ASH SYSTEMS

O&M of Ash Handling Plants (AHP)

Expert operation and maintenance of dry fly ash pneumatic conveying and bottom ash wet slurry systems for stringent environmental compliance.

Pneumatic Systems: Dense phase conveying vessels, dome valves, instrument air compressors, and silo bag filters.
Wet Slurry: Submerged Scraper Chain Conveyors (SSCC), clinker grinders, and ash slurry pump pipelines.
Request Proposal for This Service
04 FUEL HANDLING

Coal Handling Plants & Balance of Plants (BOP)

Heavy industrial fuel management from rail rakes to boiler bunkers. We ensure zero bunker starvation, continuous crusher uptime, and automated dust suppression throughout conveyor corridors.

Equipment Maintained: Wagon tipplers, ring granulator crushers, magnetic separators, motorized trippers, stacker-reclaimers.
Safety Protocols: Belt sway switches, pull-cord alignment, foam dust suppression, and coal yard compaction.
Request Proposal for This Service
05 EMISSION CONTROL

O&M of Flue Gas Desulphurization (FGD) Systems

Comprehensive Operation & Maintenance services for Flue Gas Desulphurization systems to meet stringent environmental standards and SOx emissions guidelines.

Scrubber Operations: Limestone ball mill grinding, absorber spray nozzles, mist eliminators, oxidation air blowers.
By-Product Handling: Vacuum belt filters, hydrocyclones, and gypsum cake commercial handling.
Request Proposal for This Service
06 BTG INTEGRATION

O&M of BTG, BOP & Auxiliaries

Ensuring peak performance for your core Boiler, Turbine, Generator triad, high-pressure feed pumps, cooling towers, and DM water treatment plants.

High-Pressure Boilers: CFBC, AFBC, and Pulverized Coal (PC) boilers, deaerator, and Boiler Feed Pump (BFP) operation.
Water & Draft: DM/RO water treatment plants, cooling tower chemical treatment, and ID/FD/PA fans.
Request Proposal for This Service
07 PLANNED OUTAGES

Overhaul Services: Capital (COH) & Annual (AOH)

Capital Overhauling (COH) and Annual Planned Outages (AOH) carried out with tight shutdown schedules and strict OEM tolerances.

Boiler Overhauls: Tube thickness survey (UTM), superheater replacement, hydro-testing, safety valve floating.
Turbine Inspections: Casing lifting, diaphragm blasting, NDT testing, dynamic balancing, and re-commissioning.

08 BIO-ENERGY UTILITIES

O&M of Grain-Based Distillery Plants

Turnkey utility operations for grain and molasses distilleries, including multi-effect evaporators (MEE), slop-fired incineration boilers, and cogeneration turbines for Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD).

Evaporation & Drying: Falling film MEE systems, foul condensates treatment, decanter centrifuges, DDGS rotary dryers.
Incineration Boilers: Spent wash incineration boilers, bagasse/biomass co-firing, extraction turbine generation.

09 EPC COMMISSIONING

Erection & Commissioning of Power Plants (up to 200 MW)

Heavy mechanical erection, boiler drum lifting, alloy piping, pre-commissioning chemical flushes, steam blowing, and full-load grid synchronization for plants up to 200 MW capacity.

Heavy Erection: Structure fabrication, pressure parts, high-pressure header positioning, turbine sole plate leveling.
Pre-Commissioning: Alkali boil-out, steam blowing target plate verification, safety valve floating, COD trials.
